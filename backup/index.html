PROBLEM

Carbon requests are now part of winning the deal.

PCFs have moved from a sustainability nice-to-have into RFPs and purchasing conditions. That leaves both sides stuck. Your customers ask for footprints you calculate by hand in spreadsheets, with no quality score and no standard way to send them. Your suppliers get the same request from you — and rarely answer in a format you can use.

Enviraan closes both gaps. Collect the supplier data, calculate the footprint, rate how solid it is, and publish it where partners can pull it straight into their own systems.

WHAT ENVIRAAN DOES

One end-to-end platform for secure footprint management.

Pillar 01 — CALCULATE

Turn a bill of materials into a defensible PCF.

Import your product and its components, capture supplier inputs, and Enviraan computes the footprint across every lifecycle stage — materials, production, packaging, logistics, and end-of-life. Cradle-to-gate or cradle-to-grave, on the Catena-X v9 model, with the breakdown shown stage by stage instead of a single black-box number.

Pillar 02 — EXCHANGE

Publish once. Let the network request it.

Every footprint becomes a Catena-X digital twin (Asset Administration Shell submodel) that partners can discover and consume directly. Inbound PCF requests from your customers land in Enviraan automatically, so you respond to the dataspace instead of replying to emails.

Underpinning both — DATA QUALITY RATING

A number your customers can audit.

Every PCF carries a PACT-aligned quality score — technological, temporal, and geographical representativeness, plus completeness and reliability. Your customers don't just get a number; they get a number they can trust. That score is what makes the footprint worth sharing.

HOW IT WORKS

Seamless integration, compliant outputs.

01

Request

Import a product and its BOM. Enviraan maps the components and sends standard-specific data requests to the right suppliers.

02

Respond

Suppliers complete a guided questionnaire matched to the required methodology. Enviraan calculates the footprint and assigns its quality rating.

03

Share

Publish the finished PCF as a Catena-X digital twin — downstream to customers, auditors, and your own reporting, in the format they expect.
